Drake Music:
Disability-Led
Music Technology
Organization  Drake Music is a UK-based organization working at the intersection of music, disability, and technology. It reframes accessibility as creative infrastructure, not accommodation.
Design Justice
Sasha Costanza-Chock
MIT Press, 2020
Book  Design Justice connects design to larger struggles for collective liberation. It argues for community-led design practices that redistribute power, going beyond “design for good” and decorative inclusion.
Crip-Hacking
& Crip-Aesthetics
in XR
Research  Crip-hacking reframes disability not as a deficit to be corrected, but as a source of technical invention, adaptation, and aesthetic knowledge. Mouth gestures as interface. DIY adaptation as design.
