Non-Normative
Interfaces
Non-Normative Interfaces gathers projects, tools, writings, and systems that question the assumptions built into interaction design: the able-bodied user, the individual user, the neutral tool, the screen-based interface, the productivity-driven system, and the language-first AI paradigm.

These entrances include disability-led music technologies, switch access systems, slow and reflective design frameworks, tangible interaction communities, inclusive HCI research, design justice, crip technoscience, and feminist theories of situated knowledge.
