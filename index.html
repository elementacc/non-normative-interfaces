Non-Normative
Interfaces
Non-Normative Interfaces is an independent research and publishing practice led under the creative direction of NNI, operating across digital media, interaction history, network cultures, and the margins of human-computer relations.

With a focus on the unconventional and the overlooked, NNI aims to surface, study, and distribute works that challenge normative assumptions about how screens and systems are used.
